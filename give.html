Other Ways to Give

Beyond the offering basket — here are all the ways you can sow into the Glory Zone vision.

In-Person Offering

Drop your tithe, offering, or seed into the offering basket every Sunday during either service. Every seed sown in faith is honoured by God.

Mobile Transfer
