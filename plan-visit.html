Good to Know

What to Expect

We want your first Sunday to be free from anxiety and full of expectation. Here's what a typical Sunday looks like.

🎵

Powerful Worship

We open with live worship led by Glory Sound — our worship team. Expect an atmosphere that is warm, Spirit-filled, and deeply personal. You can engage however feels right for you.

📖

The Word Preached

Our pastors preach the uncompromised Word of God with passion and clarity. Messages are biblical, practical, and life-changing. Bring your Bible or use your phone.

👶

Little Champions

Our children's ministry runs during every Sunday service. Little Champions is a safe, fun, and Spirit-filled environment where children aged 0–12 worship, learn, and pray.

👋

Warm Welcome

Our welcome team will greet you at the door. If it's your first time, let us know — we have a special gift and a personal tour of the church just for you.

👗

Come As You Are

There is no dress code at Glory Zone. Some dress up, some come casual — both are equally welcome. God looks at the heart. So do we.

⏱️

Service Length

A typical Sunday service runs between 90 and 120 minutes. We do not rush the presence of God, but we are also mindful of your time.

Register below and we'll make sure someone is ready to welcome you personally. Takes less than a minute.

🎉

You're Registered!

We can't wait to see you. Our welcome team has been notified and will be looking out for you. Come expecting an encounter with God.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there parking available? +

Yes, we have parking available near our facility at British America Junction. Our parking team will be on hand to guide you. We recommend arriving 10–15 minutes before the service begins, especially for the 10:00am service.

What should I wear? +

Come as you are! There is no dress code at Glory Zone. You will see people in Sunday best and others in casual wear. What matters is your heart, not your outfit. We want you to be comfortable from the moment you arrive.

What about my children during the service? +

Our Little Champions children's ministry runs during every Sunday service for children aged 0–12. Your children will worship, learn, and play in a completely safe and supervised environment. Our trained children's team will take great care of them.

Do I need to bring a Bible? +

Bringing your Bible is always welcome, but it is not required. Our pastors project Scriptures during the message. You can also follow along on your phone. We will never make you feel awkward for not having one.

Will I be asked to give money? +

As a first-time visitor, there is absolutely no pressure to give. We practice generosity as an act of worship, but giving is always voluntary. We encourage first-timers to simply receive — you are our guest.

I'm not a Christian — is this service right for me? +

Absolutely. Everyone is welcome at Glory Zone — whatever your background, faith history, or questions. Many people attend our services while still figuring out what they believe. You will never be singled out or made to feel uncomfortable. Come with an open heart.
