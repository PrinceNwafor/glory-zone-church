The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God. It is the final rule of faith and practice for every believer. Every truth we preach, every life we build, is grounded in Scripture.

02

The One True God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons â€” Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. He is sovereign, all-knowing, all-powerful, and ever-present. The God of glory is the God we worship and serve.

03

Jesus Christ â€” Lord and Saviour

We believe in the virgin birth, sinless life, atoning death, bodily resurrection, and glorious ascension of Jesus Christ. He is the Son of God, the only Saviour of humanity, and the Lord of all creation. He is coming again.

04

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the person and work of the Holy Spirit â€” including the gifts and manifestations of the Spirit as described in 1 Corinthians 12. The Holy Spirit is the source of supernatural power, conviction, and glory in the life of every believer.

05

Salvation by Grace Through Faith

We believe that salvation is a gift from God, received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone. No works or human merit can earn it. Every person who confesses and believes is born again, made new, and declared righteous before God.

06

Water Baptism & The Lord's Supper

We practice believer's baptism by immersion as an outward declaration of inward transformation. We celebrate the Lord's Supper as a remembrance of Christ's sacrifice and a proclamation of His death until He comes.

07

The Church â€” Body of Christ

We believe the Church is the body of Christ on earth â€” called to worship, fellowship, discipleship, ministry, and mission. Glory Zone exists as a local expression of this global body, deployed to bring the Kingdom to Jos and beyond.

The Return of Christ & Eternal Life

We believe in the literal, bodily, and imminent return of Jesus Christ. At His return, the dead in Christ shall rise and the living shall be caught up to meet Him. Eternal life awaits those who believe; we live now in the light of that glorious hope.
